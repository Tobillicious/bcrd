# FINAL CONTRIBUTION STATEMENT

One-particle-loss immunity and its syndrome-factor structure are established QEC/DFS results. In the present finite BCRD constructions, we place that reversible channel structure in a common record-dynamics formulation and obtain exact fixed per-`N` CPTP updates for the reviewed `J2/J4/J6` records because those records remain reconstructive. The contribution is therefore a finite-model synthesis and application: it distinguishes autonomy inherited from reversible encoding from the separate problem of autonomous closure after genuine information loss, without presenting the underlying factorization, recovery completion, or left-inverse-induced update as new general channel mathematics.

## Prior art / standard consequences

- collective-DFS one-particle-loss immunity;
- equivalent retained-support / syndrome factorization;
- exact QEC recovery and arbitrary-reference extension;
- routine full-algebra CPTP decoder completion;
- the induced update from a CPTP left inverse.

## BCRD-specific synthesis / application

- interpretation of the present record hierarchy through the reconstructive-versus-genuinely-lossy distinction;
- exact finite `J2/J4/J6` realization at `N=8,10,12`, with a fixed update separately at each `N`.
