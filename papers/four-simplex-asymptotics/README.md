## Scope

The paper establishes a Riemannian, leading-large-spin, single-vertex
asymptotic result for coherent nondegenerate boundary data.

It does **not** establish:

- a continuum or refinement limit;
- control of the bulk spin sum;
- Lorentzian dynamics;
- nonlinear gravitational dynamics;
- a derivation of four-dimensional general relativity.

An earlier inference from the single-vertex result to graviton dynamics is
withdrawn in the published Version 2 text; the separate glued-W2 analysis
addresses the gluing question.
