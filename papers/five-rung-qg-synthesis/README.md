## Result and scope

The paper composes five interfaces: an orientation-resolved finite-spin four-simplex amplitude; a controlled oriented Regge saddle; native full-intertwiner weak shape matching; recovery of the local length-geometric sector on the selected branch; and a quadratic DK-lattice reduction whose leading two-derivative metric operator is the linearised Einstein--Hilbert operator.

The resulting programme-level statement is scope-bound: BCRD contains a controlled four-dimensional quantum-geometric semiclassical sector whose leading metric dynamics are those of linearised general relativity in the stated regime.

The theorem is restricted to Riemannian/Euclidean signature, coherent nondegenerate boundary data, admissible large-spin sequences, the near-flat fluctuation ball, a selected local area-to-length branch, the regular or centrally subdivided Dittrich--Kogios setting, and quadratic order.

It does **not** claim a complete Lorentzian theory, nonlinear continuum Einstein dynamics, arbitrary-triangulation independence, renormalisation, universal finiteness, a nonperturbative continuum fixed point, or an independently constructed BCRD graviton propagator.
