## ESTABLISHED REVIEWED SCIENCE

- **K=4 physical common-map existence.** A whole-algebra, source-independent, time-independent physical CPTP witness `Phi_4` satisfies all four exposed transitions below `epsilon=0.01`.
- **Prospective frozen-Phi4 failure.** The exact frozen `Phi_4` fails both pristine `F1` and `F2`, with reviewed lowers `0.6246936110454476` and `0.6217658417777687`.
- **K=6 unchanged-class feasibility.** The same full physical map class remains feasible after `F1,F2` are added as constraints. Reviewed bracket: `0.002306082156934015... <= epsilon_*^(6) <= 0.004582638648933954... < 0.01`.
- **Feasible-set nesting.** Because `D_4 subset D_6` with unchanged class/tolerance, `F_6(0.01) subseteq F_4(0.01)`.
- **At least two distinct K=4 tolerance-feasible maps.** `Phi_6 in F_6`, hence `Phi_6 in F_4`; `Phi_4 in F_4` but `Phi_4 notin F_6`; therefore `Phi_4 != Phi_6`.

## INTERPRETIVE / TERMINOLOGICAL

- **Finite-data non-identification:** the K=4 data do not uniquely identify a tolerance-feasible physical map.
- **Set-membership ambiguity / non-uniqueness of the tolerance-feasible model set:** equivalent language for the same reviewed deduction.
- **Compatibility vs identification vs prediction:** existence of a common map, uniqueness/stability of the finite feasible set, and prospective performance of a selected witness are distinct questions.
- **Frozen-witness failure is not map-class failure.**

The generic possibility of non-unique compatible model sets under finite/incomplete data is established prior art and is not claimed as BCRD novelty.
