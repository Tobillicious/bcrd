## Direct incomplete-quantum-process literature

### M. Ziman (2008)
**“Incomplete quantum process tomography and principle of maximal entropy.”** Physical Review A 78, 032118. DOI `10.1103/PhysRevA.78.032118`.

Relevant positioning: incomplete information about a quantum process need not select a unique compatible channel; a maximum-entropy principle is introduced to select an estimate from the compatible possibilities.

### Teo et al. (2011)
**“Adaptive schemes for incomplete quantum process tomography.”** Physical Review A 84, 062125. DOI `10.1103/PhysRevA.84.062125`.

Relevant positioning: incomplete quantum-process data do not uniquely specify the process; an additional maximum-likelihood/maximum-entropy selection procedure is used to obtain a unique estimator.

These references are now engaged directly in the manuscript. The paper explicitly states that BCRD did **not** invent incomplete-QPT non-uniqueness or selection from a compatible CPTP set.
