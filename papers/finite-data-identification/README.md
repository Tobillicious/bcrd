# Finite-Data Underidentification of Effective Quantum Coarse Dynamics

**Author:** Tobias Croydon-McRae  
**Status:** Manuscript in preparation  
**Paper ID:** `PAPER-BCRD-FINITE-DATA-IDENTIFICATION-001`

## Summary

This paper studies the distinction between finite common-map compatibility and predictive identification of effective quantum dynamics.

In the reviewed BCRD N=8 coarse-record setting, a single physical CPTP map fitted an initial finite transition set but subsequently failed strongly on genuinely unseen transitions.

After those prospective failures were added as constraints, the full physical map class nevertheless remained compatible with all six transitions.

The reviewed six-transition minimax bracket is

\[
0.002306082156934015\ldots
\le
\epsilon_*^{(6)}
\le
0.004582638648933954\ldots
<
0.01.
\]

The central question is therefore not simply whether a compatible effective map exists, but whether finite transition data identify a map with stable predictive content.

## Scope

The paper concerns finite sampled common-map compatibility and finite-data identification.

It does not establish:

- a unique effective map;
- arbitrary-time autonomous dynamics;
- a semigroup;
- memorylessness;
- non-Markovianity;
- cross-system-size persistence;
- a continuum limit;
- gravity or quantum gravity.

## Reproducibility

The final manuscript will expose the reviewed transition set, physical map class, prospective frozen-witness test, six-transition certificate, and exact repository provenance required to reproduce the published claims.
