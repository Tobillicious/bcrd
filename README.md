# Boundary-Complete Relational Dynamics (BCRD)

**Boundary-Complete Relational Dynamics (BCRD)** is a research programme studying relational quantum dynamics, recoupling, spin-foam quantum geometry, and controlled gravitational limits.

This repository is the **curated public scientific record**. It contains publication manuscripts, reviewed results, reproducibility artifacts, release metadata, and frozen provenance associated with publicly reported claims. The active development archive is maintained separately and is not mirrored here.

## Latest paper release

### Boundary-Complete Relational Dynamics as a Four-Dimensional Quantum-Gravity Model

**Subtitle:** *A Five-Rung Derivation to the Linearised Einstein Sector in a Coherent Near-Flat Riemannian Regime*  
**Figshare item DOI:** https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33943363  
**Frozen version:** Version 1  
**Version-specific DOI:** https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33943363.v1  
**Published:** 20 September 2026

The paper composes five independently tested interfaces: a finite-spin orientation-resolved four-simplex amplitude; a controlled oriented Regge saddle; native full-intertwiner weak shape matching; recovery of the local length-geometric sector on the selected branch; and a quadratic Dittrich--Kogios reduction whose leading two-derivative metric operator is the linearised Einstein--Hilbert operator.

Within the explicit scope of the theorem, the paper supports describing BCRD as a **four-dimensional quantum-gravity model**. The frozen claim is restricted to a Riemannian/Euclidean, coherent nondegenerate, admissible large-spin, near-flat fluctuation-ball, selected-local-branch, regular/centrally-subdivided DK, quadratic regime.

It does **not** claim a complete Lorentzian theory, nonlinear continuum Einstein dynamics, arbitrary-triangulation independence, universal finiteness, renormalisation, a nonperturbative continuum fixed point, or an independently constructed BCRD graviton propagator.

## Research status and scope discipline

The 20 September 2026 synthesis is the current programme-level gravitational claim. Its conclusion is deliberately model-level and scope-bound: BCRD contains a controlled four-dimensional quantum-geometric semiclassical sector whose leading metric dynamics are those of linearised general relativity in the stated regime. It is not presented as a complete final theory of quantum gravity.

Negative results, open conjectures, withdrawals, and scope ceilings are retained as part of the scientific record.

## Public/private separation

Only frozen, publication-relevant material is promoted to this repository. Exploratory branches, preregistrations, scratch calculations, provider transcripts, and unfinished development lanes remain in the separate development archive.

The public release records **snapshots and provenance, not private development history**.
