# Boundary-Complete Relational Dynamics (BCRD)

**Boundary-Complete Relational Dynamics (BCRD)** is a finite quantum-dynamics research programme studying when relationally accessible records inside closed quantum systems can support effective dynamics of their own.

This repository is the public scientific record for BCRD. It contains publication manuscripts, reviewed results, reproducibility artifacts, and frozen provenance associated with publicly reported claims.

## Current archival release

**Figshare DOI:** https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33716677

The current public release records reviewed results in finite quantum dynamics, locality and transport, reconstructive closure, effective coarse-record dynamics, and operational Racah recoupling.

**Quantum gravity is not established.**

## Research status

BCRD distinguishes carefully between:

- finite common-map compatibility and predictive identification;
- reconstructive closure and genuinely lossy coarse-graining;
- mathematical CPTP maps and physically declared interventions;
- Racah / \(6j^2\) probabilities and coherent recoupling amplitudes;
- quantum-geometric mathematical structure and quantum gravity.

Negative results and scope ceilings are retained as part of the scientific record.
